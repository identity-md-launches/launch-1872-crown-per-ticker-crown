How it works

  1. One king per ticker. The registry records which token is the canonical one for a symbol and which wallet gets paid for it.
  2. Swaps pay the king. Pools wearing the Crown hook take a small cut of the ETH side of every trade (1% on the demo pool, never more than 5%) and send it to the king's wallet in the same transaction. Nothing is held by the hook; if it cannot pay, the trade still goes through without the cut.
  3. Anyone can challenge. After a king has reigned 7 days, anyone can name a rival token and post a 0.01 ETH bond. That opens a 72-hour window where anyone can stake ETH for the king or for the challenger.
  4. Then a 24-hour timelock. After it, anyone can finalize. The challenger wins only if its side staked more than the king's side and at least 0.05 ETH was staked in total.
  5. Winners and losers. Winning stakers get all their ETH back plus a share of the loser's slash. Losers get 80% back; 20% is slashed, half to the winners and half to the treasury. Everyone withdraws their own money with "Claim".
  6. Failed challenge. The ticker cannot be challenged again for 3 days.
